[00:08:44] Host: What does the industry get most wrong right now?
[00:09:20] Elena Vasquez: Treating grid modernization as an engineering story when it's a cost-recovery story. Everyone talks about the technology — sensors, self-healing feeders, AMI. A brilliant program on a bad rider is a bad deal. A modest program on a well-designed performance mechanism is a good one. The engineering press covers the sensors. [00:14:10] Host: Biggest thing you got wrong early?

[00:16:55] Host: Where does AI fit?
