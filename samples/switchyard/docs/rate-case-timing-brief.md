# Switchyard Rate-Case Timing Brief (internal)

## Summary
This brief quantifies regulatory lag across the major electric rate cases Switchyard tracks. It is an internal evidence document — supporting data for our published thought leadership, not itself a public statement.

## Filing-to-decision timing
Across the last 60 major rate cases in our dataset, the median time from initial filing to new rates taking effect is 11.2 months. The 25th percentile is 8 months; the 90th percentile stretches to 17 months for fully litigated cases. Settled cases resolve about 3 months faster than litigated ones.

## Settlement rate
72 percent of major rate cases in the dataset settled before a full litigated commission decision. The settlement rate has risen from about 58 percent a decade ago.

## Authorized ROE trend
Authorized returns on equity have declined from a decade-ago average near 10.5 percent to roughly 9.6 percent in the trailing twelve months. The decline correlates with the ten-year Treasury at an R-squared above 0.8 across the period.

## Rider proliferation
The median number of separate line-item riders on a residential bill rose from 3.5 a decade ago to 13 today. Grid-modernization and storm-recovery riders account for most of the growth.
