How the workbench works

One job: turn what your people actually said into publishable content, and turn your editing taste into enforceable rules. The four views are the four stages of that assembly line.

1 Corpus — the source material

Everything the system can draw from lives here, scoped to this workspace. Drop in documents (PDF, Word, Markdown, text), paste raw text, or add a public URL — each is segmented into provenance-linked passages that extraction and weaving cite as receipts. Transcripts, owned documents, and public web pages are tagged by class; the corpus is a hard workspace boundary — nothing crosses between clients.

2 Slate — what's worth publishing today?

Your daily queue. The system mines your transcripts for moments — publishable insights someone actually said — ranks them, and shows only the top few (the header's attention-budget bar is that promise). Per card: Weave it into content, or Reject it with a typed reason. Rejections are training signal — the system learns your taste from what you kill.

3 Drafts — the content, with receipts

Weaving produces drafts: posts, blogs, threads, clip specs. Every claim-bearing sentence carries a receipt — the monospace chip naming who said it, where, and when; click it to unfold the transcript. Nothing ships that a real human didn't say. "Edit as the human editor" captures your corrections as diffs: your edit becomes policy.

4 Constitution — your taste, made into law

Distill turns recurring edits into candidate principles ("cut throat-clearing openers"). You never ratify a rule because it sounds right — Ratify shows what the rule would have done to your recent drafts, as diffs. If the blast radius (out-of-scope drafts changed) exceeds 10%, Accept locks. Lifecycle, enforced server-side: candidate → shadow (a week of observation) → active.

5 Gate — is this actually working?

The Phase 0 scoreboard against pre-registered thresholds (acceptance ≥60%, edit recurrence ↓≥30%, blast <10%, coverage ≥50%). ⬜ means not yet measurable — honest, not broken.

The loop: corpus → slate → weave → edit → distill → ratify → constitution grows → drafts come out closer to right → you edit less. Every hour of editing judgment becomes permanent infrastructure. Full guide: docs/UI-GUIDE.md.
